# Eat Your Feelings

Become someone your cat respects. Maybe raise the dead. Heck, you can even develop a personality without doing any actual work.

At Sprinkles and Spells, help a foul-tempered fairy build magical layered cakes that turn customers’ bad decisions into someone else’s problem!

Listen to their questionable wishes, decipher the clues in six peculiar ingredients, and discover which recipes deliver the desired miracles.

Layers of cake for the mistakes you make!
